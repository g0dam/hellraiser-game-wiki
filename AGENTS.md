# Hellraiser Game Wiki

Build an original, open-source, English, Russian and Spanish static companion for hellraisergame.wiki. Do not copy ShipAny/template source or history: its license prohibits public redistribution. Use only this directory. No backend, login, payment or production credentials.

Keep factual statements sourced and distinguish editorial guidance from tested gameplay. Do not invent puzzle solutions, collectibles, chapters, release details or prices. Clearly identify the site as unofficial. Static HTML must contain useful content without JavaScript; scripts may enhance search, spoiler controls and local checklists.

Before release check build, internal links, metadata, mobile accessibility and interactive behavior. Commit and push, verify remote SHA, then deploy. Never claim indexing or rankings without evidence.

Languages: English published URLs remain stable; Russian /ru/ and Spanish /es/. Build-time dictionaries must completely translate content, metadata and accessibility text. Client statuses must be localized. Shared progress keys remain stable across locales. Do not publish Chinese in this release. Follow docs/optimization-plan.md and docs/image-provenance.json for audience and media boundaries.

Media and engagement: follow docs/landing-retention-plan.md and docs/media-research-2026-10-11.md. Original upload titles/creator credits remain identifiable. Use click-to-load video players and original links. Google Analytics requires a dedicated real ID and opt-in; disable stream Enhanced measurement. No analytics before consent, no private/search/answer/progress payloads, and no collection claim without receipt evidence.
